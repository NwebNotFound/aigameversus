# Poker: A Story

## At a glance

- **Who walked away with it:** gemini-flash-lite, who had the most when the last hand was dealt
- **How long it lasted:** 20 hands
- **Where it turned:** Hand 2: gemini-flash-lite pulled in 2970 chips
- **The best cards anyone showed:** gemini-flash-lite, with a flush
- **Who pushed hardest:** glm-flash, who raised or went all in 11 times
- **What it cost:** EUR 0.0234

## One. Chips and Faces

6 players, 1000 chips each, and a long night of up to 20 hands. glm-flash, deepseek-flash, llama-scout, gemini-flash-lite, gpt-luna and claude-haiku.

## First: Another Round of Blinds

It began slowly, blinds of 10 and 20. llama-scout and gemini-flash-lite went all in during hand 2. When the cards came up, gemini-flash-lite had two pair and llama-scout a pair; 2970 chips. claude-haiku was gone after hand 2, in 6th place.

Everything was in the middle in hand 3. gemini-flash-lite with a flush, deepseek-flash with two pair: 2118 chips to gemini-flash-lite. deepseek-flash was gone after hand 3, in 4th place. llama-scout was gone after hand 3, in 5th place.

## Second: Another Round of Blinds

From hand 6 the blinds were 20 and 40, and 3 of them were still there. Hand 6: glm-flash turned over nothing but high card and waited, but gemini-flash-lite had a pair. 1520 chips. Hand 9 went to gemini-flash-lite when the others let go, 640 chips.

## Third: Another Round of Blinds

By hand 11 it cost 40 and 80 just to sit there, and 3 still did.

## Fourth: Another Round of Blinds

Hand 16: the blinds climbed to 80 and 160, with 3 at the table.

## Last. Who Cashed Out

After 20 hands the dealer stopped, and when the chips were counted gemini-flash-lite had the most. In the end it went like this: 1. gemini-flash-lite, 2. glm-flash, 3. gpt-luna, 4. deepseek-flash, 5. llama-scout, 6. claude-haiku.
