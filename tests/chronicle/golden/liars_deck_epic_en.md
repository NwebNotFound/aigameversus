# The Saga of Liar's Deck

## At a glance

- **Last one standing:** glm-flash
- **Duration:** 10 rounds
- **The turning point:** Round 9: gemini-flash-lite faced a coin flip with the revolver and lived
- **Luckiest soul:** gemini-flash-lite, who lived through 5 pulls of the trigger
- **Cost of the saga:** EUR 0.0494

## Prologue: The Table Is Set

4 players sat down at the table, each with a revolver and a lie in reserve: glm-flash, deepseek-flash, llama-scout and gemini-flash-lite.

## The Round of the First Trial

The first round began beneath the banner of the king, with 4 still at the table, and glm-flash opened. llama-scout pushed 3 cards forward and swore they were all kings; 2 were lies. gemini-flash-lite said: "Three kings, for the crown!" gemini-flash-lite played 3 kings and told the truth, which was the boldest bluff of all. "Nine kings claimed, but the deck holds only six." said glm-flash. glm-flash did not believe gemini-flash-lite, and said so. The cards turned over: king, king, joker. All true to the king. glm-flash had accused an honest hand. glm-flash pulled the trigger, pull 1, odds 1 in 6. Click. The chamber was empty.

## The Round of the Second Trial

The cards were dealt anew, and the ace ruled the second round; glm-flash spoke first. "Two real aces on top of yours." said deepseek-flash. "Liar!" cried llama-scout, pointing at deepseek-flash. The truth lay on the table: ace, ace. llama-scout had doubted the wrong player. Pull 1 was the live one for llama-scout, and the table fell silent. So llama-scout finished 4th, and the chair stayed empty.

## The Round of the Third Trial

The third round: the queen held sway, 3 remained, and deepseek-flash began the talking. glm-flash offered the table this: "Three queens, all genuine." glm-flash laid 3 cards down, and every one of them really was a queen. deepseek-flash said: "Three queens with none in my hand?" deepseek-flash did not believe glm-flash, and said so. The truth lay on the table: queen, queen, joker. deepseek-flash had doubted the wrong player. deepseek-flash pulled the trigger, and the thunderclap said it all. deepseek-flash was gone. deepseek-flash left the table in 3rd place.

## The Round of the Fourth Trial

The fourth round began beneath the banner of the queen, with 2 still at the table, and glm-flash opened. Alone at the table, glm-flash and gemini-flash-lite measured each other once more. "Three queens to open, straight from the top of the deck." said glm-flash. glm-flash pushed 3 cards forward and swore they were all queens; 3 were lies. gemini-flash-lite spoke, calm as a lake: "I don't believe you played three queens." gemini-flash-lite narrowed their eyes at glm-flash: liar. glm-flash's hand lay open: ace, king, king. The claim of queens collapsed, and gemini-flash-lite had been right. glm-flash put the barrel to the temple, pull 2, and heard only a click.

## The Round of the Fifth Trial

The fifth round: the queen held sway, 2 remained, and glm-flash began the talking. glm-flash offered the table this: "A single honest queen." gemini-flash-lite did not believe glm-flash, and said so. The truth lay on the table: queen. gemini-flash-lite had doubted the wrong player. gemini-flash-lite put the barrel to the temple, pull 1, and heard only a click.

## The Round of the Sixth Trial

The sixth round: the king held sway, 2 remained, and gemini-flash-lite began the talking. gemini-flash-lite pushed 3 cards forward and swore they were all kings; 3 were lies. glm-flash offered the table this: "Two honest kings on top of your three." "Liar!" cried gemini-flash-lite, pointing at glm-flash. glm-flash's cards said king, king: honest to the last. gemini-flash-lite had misjudged. Pull 2 for gemini-flash-lite, at 1 in 5, and only silence answered.

## The Round of the Seventh Trial

Round 7: the table card was the queen, 2 players remained, and gemini-flash-lite led. "One honest queen, again." said glm-flash. "One honest queen, huh?" said gemini-flash-lite. gemini-flash-lite rose and called glm-flash a liar! The cards turned over: queen. All true to the queen. gemini-flash-lite had accused an honest hand. gemini-flash-lite put the barrel to the temple, pull 3, and heard only a click.

## The Round of the Eighth Trial

The eighth round began beneath the banner of the king, with 2 still at the table, and gemini-flash-lite opened. glm-flash offered the table this: "One king, only one." gemini-flash-lite spoke, calm as a lake: "You claim one king?" Once more: gemini-flash-lite rose and called glm-flash a liar! glm-flash showed king, and it was all honest. gemini-flash-lite had called wrongly. gemini-flash-lite pulled, pull 4, odds 1 in 3, and the chamber was empty. A narrow escape.

## The Round of the Ninth Trial

The cards were dealt anew, and the queen ruled the ninth round; gemini-flash-lite spoke first. gemini-flash-lite spoke, calm as a lake: "You say two queens, glm-flash?" Once more: gemini-flash-lite did not believe glm-flash, and said so. The truth lay on the table: queen, queen. A 1 in 2 chance of death, and gemini-flash-lite lived: pull 5, and the cylinder turned on.

## The Round of the Tenth Trial

The tenth round: the queen held sway, 2 remained, and gemini-flash-lite began the talking. glm-flash said: "Three honest queens, gemini-flash-lite." glm-flash played 3 queens and told the truth, which was the boldest bluff of all. Yet again: gemini-flash-lite did not believe glm-flash, and said so. glm-flash showed queen, queen, queen, and it was all honest. gemini-flash-lite pulled the trigger, and the thunderclap said it all. gemini-flash-lite was gone. gemini-flash-lite left the table in 2nd place.

## Epilogue: The Last at the Table

glm-flash alone remained after 10 rounds, the last one breathing. The final order: 1. glm-flash, 2. gemini-flash-lite, 3. deepseek-flash, 4. llama-scout.
