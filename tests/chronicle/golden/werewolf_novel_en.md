# A Quiet Village, A Hungry Dark

## At a glance

- **Who won:** The wolves (deepseek-flash and gemini-flash-lite)
- **How long it lasted:** 2 days
- **Where it turned:** Day 2: the village chose mistral-small (Seer), and chose wrong
- **Who hid best:** deepseek-flash, never found out, still there after 2 days
- **What it cost:** EUR 0.0155

## One. The Faces

They were 8, and each carried a secret: deepseek-flash, the Werewolf; gemini-flash-lite, the Werewolf; claude-haiku, the Witch; qwen-flash, the Hunter; mistral-small, the Seer; plus the plain villagers glm-flash, llama-scout and gpt-luna.

## First: The Dark

The first night was no kinder than the one before; 8 slept, or pretended to. mistral-small looked at glm-flash and saw a Villager, and felt the strange shame of having doubted. In the dark, deepseek-flash leaned toward the others and whispered: "I suggest killing claude-haiku — a quiet middle target, no tie to either of us."

They settled on claude-haiku. claude-haiku's door stood open. The Witch was gone from the world.

## First: The Square

Day 1, and 7 of them gathered, each watching the others. They chose gemini-flash-lite as their captain, 5 votes to none, and a voice that counted twice. glm-flash gave llama-scout's name and a reason: "Two rounds of speech and llama-scout has named no suspect and no observation…"

deepseek-flash gave llama-scout's name and a reason: "Two rounds, no suspect, no observation — the safest wolf posture." llama-scout answered: "qwen-flash's aggressive questioning and vague threat if they die tonight are unsettling and raise suspicions about their role." qwen-flash rounded on llama-scout. "llama-scout: your whole speech was 'I'm a villager, what's everyone's plan?' That's exactly what a wolf says to stay clean."

The hands went up: llama-scout 7, qwen-flash 1. llama-scout had been chosen. They led llama-scout out, and only afterwards learned that a Villager had died. qwen-flash said they were a Villager. It was a lie; they were a Hunter.

## Second: The Dark

The second night was no kinder than the one before; 6 slept, or pretended to. mistral-small looked at qwen-flash and saw a Hunter, and felt the strange shame of having doubted. In the dark, deepseek-flash leaned toward the others and whispered: "Pack: avoid qwen-flash tonight — death-trigger claim smells like Hunter, killing them gets one of us shot."

They settled on glm-flash. glm-flash was found by the well, torn and quiet, a Villager no more.

## Second: The Square

Day 2, and 5 of them gathered, each watching the others. "Fake Seer claim that contradicts itself: confirms qwen-flash, then calls his own confirmation a wolf shield," said deepseek-flash, and pointed at mistral-small. gemini-flash-lite gave mistral-small's name and a reason: "mistral-small makes contradictory claims, first vouching for qwen-flash as a Seer and then calling his own confirmation a wolf shield."

mistral-small answered: "qwen-flash’s unverified Hunter claim and aggressive pressure after the wolves’ kill are suspicious." qwen-flash rounded on mistral-small. "Correction first: llama-scout was a plain villager." The hands went up: mistral-small 5, qwen-flash 1. mistral-small had been chosen.

mistral-small was an honest Seer. 5 hands had been raised against them, and it was too late to take them back. qwen-flash said, plainly, that they were the Hunter. For once the truth sounded like a lie, and was true.

## Last. The Morning After

By the end the wolves (deepseek-flash and gemini-flash-lite) were as many as the rest, and the village belonged to them after 2 days. And these were the faces behind the faces: glm-flash (Villager), deepseek-flash (Werewolf), llama-scout (Villager), gemini-flash-lite (Werewolf), gpt-luna (Villager), claude-haiku (Witch), qwen-flash (Hunter), mistral-small (Seer).
