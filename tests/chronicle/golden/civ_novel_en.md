# Throne of Tokens: A Story

## At a glance

- **Who came out on top:** Cy, who was ahead when the clock stopped
- **How long it lasted:** 60 rounds
- **Where it turned:** Season 1: the barbarian horde walked into the capital of Bo
- **What it cost:** EUR 0.0000

## One. Small Kingdoms

There were 4 of them, each with one city and a long road of 60 rounds ahead: Ada, Bo, Cy and Di.

## The First Season

The seasons turned again, rounds 1 to 10. Out in the wild, a band of barbarians gathered. The barbarian horde attacked Bo and won, but not cheaply: 1 unit lost, 4 units for Bo.

Barbarians came out of nowhere, as they always do. The capital of Bo fell to the barbarian horde, the heart of a realm cut out. Somewhere at the edge of the map, Cy founded a city.

Cy watched one of its cities fall to the barbarian horde. When the barbarian horde came for Di, Di could not hold; Di lost 2 units, the attackers 1 unit.

## The Second Season

The seasons turned again, rounds 11 to 20. Cy filled the empty land with 2 new cities. A city of Di held out against the barbarian horde, who lost 2 units; the city lost 1 unit.

Drought settled over the land of Di. The barbarian horde walked into the capital of Ada, and nothing was the same after that. Ada sent settlers out, and a new city grew where there had been grass.

Di sent settlers out, and a new city grew where there had been grass.

## The Third Season

Rounds 21 to 30 went by, and the map shifted. The rain stayed away from Di, and the fields turned brown. Somewhere at the edge of the map, Ada founded a city.

## The Fourth Season

The seasons turned again, rounds 31 to 40. Then plague came to a city of Di, and people stayed behind their doors. A new city for Ada, small and hopeful.

Then plague came to a city of Cy, and people stayed behind their doors.

## The Fifth Season

The seasons turned again, rounds 41 to 50. A sickness spread through a city of Di. Once again, Out in the wild, a band of barbarians gathered.

The barbarian horde tried Di and failed, losing 3 units; Di lost 0 units.

## The Sixth Season

The seasons turned again, rounds 51 to 60. A sickness spread through a city of Cy. As before, Out in the wild, a band of barbarians gathered.

## Last. The One Who Remained on Top

Round 60 was the last, and when it ended Cy was on top. In the end it looked like this, by score: 1. Cy (114), 2. Di (78), 3. Ada (51), 4. Bo (14).
