# The Saga of the Throne of Tokens

## At a glance

- **Who held the throne:** Cy, the strongest when time ran out
- **Duration:** 60 rounds
- **The turning point:** Age 1: the barbarian horde took the capital of Bo
- **Cost of the saga:** EUR 0.0000

## Prologue: The Nations Awaken

4 realms woke to a world of 60 rounds and one throne: Ada, Bo, Cy and Di.

## The First Age

Rounds 1 to 10: the first age of the world. From the wild places came a barbarian horde. The barbarian horde struck Bo and carried the day, losing 1 unit while Bo lost 4 units. Barbarians gathered in the wilderness, hungry for plunder. The barbarian horde marched into the very capital of Bo. Settlers of Cy drove their stakes into new ground, and a city stood. The barbarian horde broke the walls of a city of Cy and made it theirs. The barbarian horde struck Di and carried the day, losing 1 unit while Di lost 2 units.

## The Second Age

The second age dawned, rounds 11 to 20. 2 new cities bore the banner of Cy. The barbarian horde besieged a city of Di, but its walls held; the barbarian horde lost 2 units, Di 1 unit. Drought burned the fields of Di, and the granaries ran low. The barbarian horde marched into the very capital of Ada. Ada founded a city, and its banner rose over fresh fields. Settlers of Di drove their stakes into new ground, and a city stood.

## The Third Age

The third age dawned, rounds 21 to 30. The rains failed in the lands of Di. Ada raised a new city from the wilderness.

## The Fourth Age

So began the fourth age, from round 31 to round 40. A sickness swept through a city of Di. Settlers of Ada drove their stakes into new ground, and a city stood. A sickness swept through a city of Cy.

## The Fifth Age

The fifth age dawned, rounds 41 to 50. Plague crept into a city of Di, and the streets fell silent. Once more: From the wild places came a barbarian horde. The barbarian horde attacked Di and was thrown back; the barbarian horde lost 3 units, Di 0 units.

## The Sixth Age

So began the sixth age, from round 51 to round 60. Plague crept into a city of Cy, and the streets fell silent. Yet again: From the wild places came a barbarian horde.

## Epilogue: Who Held the Throne

Round 60 came, the last of the world, and Cy stood strongest of all. The final reckoning, by score: 1. Cy (114), 2. Di (78), 3. Ada (51), 4. Bo (14).
