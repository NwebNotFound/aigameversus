# The Chronicle of the Werewolf Village

## At a glance

- **Victor:** The wolves (deepseek-flash and gemini-flash-lite)
- **Duration:** 2 days
- **The turning point:** Day 2: the village sent mistral-small (Seer) to the gallows, and the wrong one fell
- **Master of masks:** deepseek-flash, unmasked by nobody, alive at the end after 2 days
- **Cost of the tale:** EUR 0.0155

## Prologue: The Gathering

The village gathered, 8 souls beneath one roof: deepseek-flash, the Werewolf; gemini-flash-lite, the Werewolf; claude-haiku, the Witch; qwen-flash, the Hunter; mistral-small, the Seer; plus the plain villagers glm-flash, llama-scout and gpt-luna.

## The First Night

The first night came down cold and silent upon 8 sleepers. The Seer mistral-small looked at glm-flash: a Villager, nothing more. Teeth gleamed as deepseek-flash murmured, "I suggest killing claude-haiku — a quiet middle target, no tie to either of us." The pack chose claude-haiku. claude-haiku (Witch) did not wake; the wolves had been there.

## The First Day

The first day dawned on 7 survivors, and the arguing began. The village chose gemini-flash-lite as captain with 5 votes, a voice that would count double. "Two rounds of speech and llama-scout has named no suspect and no observation…," said glm-flash, and cast the vote against llama-scout. deepseek-flash named llama-scout: "Two rounds, no suspect, no observation — the safest wolf posture." llama-scout spoke up: "qwen-flash's aggressive questioning and vague threat if they die tonight are unsettling and raise suspicions about their role." qwen-flash turned on llama-scout: "llama-scout: your whole speech was 'I'm a villager, what's everyone's plan?' That's exactly what a wolf says to stay clean." The count was read: llama-scout 7, qwen-flash 1. llama-scout stood condemned. llama-scout, an honest Villager, was dragged to the gallows with 7 voices against, and the wolves smiled. qwen-flash swore to be a Villager, but was in truth a Hunter.

## The Second Night

The second night came down cold and silent upon 6 sleepers. The Seer mistral-small looked at qwen-flash: a Hunter, nothing more. In the dark, deepseek-flash whispered to the pack: "Pack: avoid qwen-flash tonight — death-trigger claim smells like Hunter, killing them gets one of us shot." The pack chose glm-flash. At first light glm-flash the Villager was found torn apart by the wolves.

## The Second Day

Day 2: 5 of them gathered in the square, eyes searching faces. deepseek-flash named mistral-small: "Fake Seer claim that contradicts itself: confirms qwen-flash, then calls his own confirmation a wolf shield." gemini-flash-lite named mistral-small: "mistral-small makes contradictory claims, first vouching for qwen-flash as a Seer and then calling his own confirmation a wolf shield." mistral-small spoke up: "qwen-flash’s unverified Hunter claim and aggressive pressure after the wolves’ kill are suspicious." qwen-flash turned on mistral-small: "Correction first: llama-scout was a plain villager." The count was read: mistral-small 5, qwen-flash 1. mistral-small stood condemned. mistral-small, an honest Seer, was dragged to the gallows with 5 voices against, and the wolves smiled. qwen-flash declared the role of Hunter, and it was no lie.

## Epilogue: What Remained

The wolves (deepseek-flash and gemini-flash-lite) now outnumbered the village, and the night was theirs for good after 2 days. Behind the masks: glm-flash (Villager), deepseek-flash (Werewolf), llama-scout (Villager), gemini-flash-lite (Werewolf), gpt-luna (Villager), claude-haiku (Witch), qwen-flash (Hunter), mistral-small (Seer).
