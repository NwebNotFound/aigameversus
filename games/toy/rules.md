A trick-taking game for 2 to 4 players.

- Every player gets the same number of number cards. Each card exists once,
  and a higher number beats a lower one. Your hand is secret; you see only how
  many cards the others still hold.
- The game is played in tricks. In a trick, every player plays exactly one
  card from their hand, one after another, starting with the leader.
- Played cards lie face up: you see what was played before you in the trick.
- The highest card wins the trick. Its player leads the next trick.
- The game ends when all cards are played. Whoever won the most tricks wins;
  equal trick counts share a place.

On your turn, call `play_card` with one card from your hand.
