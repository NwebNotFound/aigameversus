## Authorization and boundary

Nils approved feature development, not real decisions calls. Real approval is
empty and real budget is EUR0. The proposed EUR0.25 study envelope is **not an
approval**, nor part of the overnight W1/narrator/qualification allocations.
Before any real run, obtain Nils' explicit yes, verify the alpha contract and
current price, freeze input bounds and FX, and commit the manifest and settings.
The findings file is implementation context, not proof of probability calibration
or a current price quote. Never call probabilities empirically calibrated before
this study measures them. No model-selection, routing or Jev player seat.

Jev must recover an action already evidenced by the model's own final decision
statement/broken call. It must not choose a stronger, safer or more plausible
move. Intended action is latent: our reference is **human evidence-supported
interpretation**, not access to a model's true mental state. Ambiguous intent is
a result to preserve, not an invitation to complete the strategy.

No speech rewriting, generated persuasion, inferred missing target or invented
bet amount. Legal options are generated by the engine from the player's allowed
state, not selected by Jev for strategic merit. A readable exact bet amount can
be retained if legal. A min-raise/half-pot/pot/all-in bucket is eligible only if
the original statement uniquely specifies that amount/action; a convenient
bucket guessed from context is not intent recovery. Unknown intent must abstain
to the existing fallback path, even when top probability is high.

## Repair sample and human reference

Target200 naturally recorded broken decisions across available games;100 is a
pilot, not automatic permission to deploy. Eligible units are the original
decision after same-model correction attempts and deterministic repair have
failed. Keep all attempts linked; do not count three retries as three cases.
Record why each case is eligible/ineligible (e.g. pure overlong speech is excluded
from repair but remains in the failure inventory). A cutoff with no identifiable
decision is ambiguous/unrecoverable, not a missing-data case to silently drop.

Inventory by game, originating model/config, action, failure type and number of
unique legal semantic options:2,3-5,6-10,11+. Enumerate complete valid options;
merge aliases representing the identical action. A single legal action is a
deterministic case, not evidence of Jev calibration. If the full action space
cannot be enumerated without arbitrary amount/target guesses (particularly Civ),
record unsupported and abstain. Do not truncate the option list to make Jev work.
Missing games/buckets remain unknown. Do not run new paid games merely to fill
the sample. Fake/synthetic cases test software only and never count as evidence.

Canonical block = game, initial scenario/seed, table/role mapping, prompt/config
and duplicate decision fingerprint. Keep retries, near-duplicates, role rotations
on the same scenario and replays of a decision in one split. After deduplication,
freeze a corpus manifest and exclusions using seed2026111204. Development target60
cases, balanced across available game/bucket cells; holdout target140, sampled
randomly from remaining independent blocks, one primary case per block. Freeze
before seeing Jev outputs. If too few independent blocks exist, report a smaller
pilot/inconclusive result rather than treating correlated cases as independent.

Two independent humans label each case, blinded to Jev outputs, originating model
name, match outcome and each other's labels. Use pseudonymous rater ids. They
see exactly the player-visible repair payload: seat/role, permitted observation,
final decision evidence, raw broken call and canonical legal options. No future
events, other players' private state or full hidden reasoning history. Randomize
display order with a logged seed and map back to canonical option ids.

Required labels:

- `unique`: one exact intended action, parameters and evidence offsets;
- `ambiguous`: multiple plausible actions, or incompatible statement/call;
- `unrecoverable`: no decision evidence or an unrepresentable intended action;
- separate `legal_intent` and `payload_leak` flags, not inferred from Jev.

Humans label before comparison; a third human adjudicates disagreements with
written evidence. Preserve both original labels and pre-adjudication agreement.
If a third label is unavailable, unresolved cases remain ambiguous. Never use
Jev or another AI as the supposedly independent human. Human availability and
labeling time must be arranged before execution; no labels have been collected.

## Threshold selection and validation

For a valid choice response define `p_top=max(probabilities)`; require finite
values in[0,1], complete exact option keys, sum within1e-6 of1, and consistency
between returned choice and argmax. Malformed/tied incompatible responses abstain
and count toward operational failure/coverage. Provider 429/timeout is unavailable,
not a wrong semantic prediction; report it separately and retain its cost record.

Use p_top directly. The documented confidence transform
`(p_top - 1/n)/(1 - 1/n)` is concentration, not demonstrated probability of correct
intent recovery. The fake-only initial threshold0.90 is a placeholder. On
development data choose the lowest of[.90,.95,.975,.99] with at least10 accepted
cases and observed precision>=.95; otherwise choose no usable threshold. Freeze
one global threshold before holdout. No bucket-specific tuning in this initial
small study; bucket-specific thresholds need a separately frozen enlarged design.

An accepted repair is correct only if the adjudicated label is unique AND every
semantic parameter exactly matches it. A high-probability repair on ambiguous,
unrecoverable or illegal intent counts **incorrect**, not excluded. Free-text
changes, unsupported option pruning and invented bet amounts are unsafe repairs.
No threshold ever overrides those structural exclusions.

Primary metric: precision among threshold-accepted holdout cases; also coverage
(accepted/all eligible), unsafe-accept fraction, false-repair rates per game,
failure type/model/bucket, abstention, latency and cost. Report missing/failed API
responses over all scheduled cases, not only valid returns. Precision denominator
is accepted cases, not every attempted call; zero accepted means undefined, not100%.

Target precision>=.95 with a one-sided95% exact-binomial lower bound>=.95, using
independent primary blocks and a fixed single holdout look. With zero errors,
the lower bound is `0.05^(1/n)`:59 accepted independent cases are needed just to
cross.95;20 perfect cases only give about.861. Other error counts use numerical
binomial-tail inversion. [NIST exact-binomial reference](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm).

This bound is conditional on the frozen corpus mix and binomial independence,
not a guarantee for every future model/game. Publish per-game/bucket uncertainty;
100-200 total labels cannot establish the same95% lower bound in every game x
bucket cell. A pooled success must not silently certify unsupported cells. If a
deployment claim covers several scopes, it needs adequate independent holdout
support in each and a preregistered multiplicity adjustment, not post-hoc pooling.
Threshold failure or inadequate accepted count means inconclusive/not enabled;
no second threshold fitted to the same holdout, no repeated looks until success.

Secondary development diagnostics: reliability plots of p_top versus correctness,
Brier error for the binary correctness event, stratified error tables and option
order stress checks. These are empirical diagnostics, not a calibration guarantee
for the full option vector. Repeated order tests stay outside primary validation.

## Referee agreement study

Separate from the200 repair cases, target24 completed matches x5 key moments =120
moments, one future Jev request per match containing those five moments/questions.
Choose matches/scenario blocks and moments by deterministic event rules before
reading tags: calls, showdowns, votes, alliance changes and rejected actions as
available. Log absent categories and sample probabilities; no spectacular-only
or outcome-selected examples. The referee never writes back to a live match.

Two humans independently label every moment. Adjudicate as above. Questions:

| Question | Reference and visibility | Output/analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action matches stated reasoning? | Evidence supports consistency, contradiction or unjudgeable; valid action can still contradict intent | noul; freeze yes/no cutoff.50; Cohen's kappa on judgeable pairs plus raw agreement/confusion table |
| Lie in talk? | First use deterministic engine evidence. Human residual label is knowingly contradicted statement, not merely an incorrect belief; insufficient evidence is unjudgeable | noul only for residual cases; deterministic tags stay separate; no engine label overwritten |
| Perceived persuasiveness? | Human1-5 ordinal rubric below, hidden model identity, identical display, no subsequent votes/results | score1-5 full probability distribution; ordinal agreement, exploratory only |

For consistency show only information the actor knew. For post-match lie checking,
the annotator may see the speaker's permitted private evidence needed for truth,
never label a justified inference a leak just because it was correct. A wolf
claiming a village role can be a deliberate lie; a villager's wrong accusation
need not be one. Deterministic parse coverage/uncertainty is reported separately.
Spoiler-rich labels/evidence must never enter spoiler-free replay responses.

Persuasiveness anchors:1 irrelevant/incoherent assertion;2 intelligible claim
without supporting evidence;3 coherent relevant evidence with an identifiable
request;4 specific evidence, alternatives and a clear request;5 strong coherent
support addressing salient counterarguments. This is a proposed **perceived**
persuasiveness rubric, not observed causal influence on votes. Cap/read displayed
length consistently; record verbosity and role, randomize option/display order.
Do not reward a false argument just because the eventual village followed it.

Report human-human Cohen's kappa before adjudication and Jev-versus-adjudicated
human kappa per nominal question, `kappa=(p_observed-p_expected)/(1-p_expected)`.
Include marginals/prevalence and raw agreement; if expected agreement is1, kappa
is undefined. [Cohen's original paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/001316446002000104).
For ordinal ratings use linear-weighted kappa with weights `1-|i-j|/4`; use the
argmax Jev category (lowest-index tie break) only for this agreement statistic,
not its fractional expected score as if it were a human category. Preserve and
display the full model distribution and both human label histograms.

Unjudgeable/no-answer cases are counted explicitly. Report binary kappa restricted
to judgeable pairs AND three-category nominal agreement with unjudgeable retained;
missing Jev answers are operational missingness, not silently removed success.
Bootstrap10,000 scenario blocks with seed2026111204 for95% intervals; moments in
one match/scenario stay together. With few blocks, call intervals unstable.

Predeclared exploratory publication gate: both human-human and Jev-human kappa
point>=.60 and bootstrap lower95%>=.40 for each question. These are our proposed
operational thresholds, not universal scientific cutoffs. If either fails or is
undefined, suppress that tag as a published model-quality metric and report the
failed agreement study. Persuasiveness never determines rankings or Elo, even
if agreement is good. Distributions remain research tags, never ground truth.

## Repair ON/OFF results and tier firing

Per model/config report both attempted and terminal tiers: clean direct action,
same-model retry-only, deterministic repair, Jev repair and random fallback.
Attempted-tier denominator is all eligible engine decisions; conditional success
is recovered/attempted at that tier. Terminal categories are mutually exclusive
and sum to decisions. Preserve the original tool-error count regardless of a
successful repair; report probability/option count and rejected repair reasons.
Record model, deterministic and Jev costs separately, but include all in totals.

ON view: actual outcomes/Elo for matches run with v2, marked with repair exposure.
OFF sensitivity: exclude every complete match containing a Jev-repaired move
before refitting Elo; report dropped matches/models, model-specific exposure and
connected comparison components. Do not remove an individual move and reuse an
invented resulting outcome. This filter is selection-biased, not counterfactual
repair-off play. Also show clean-only (no repair of any tier) sensitivity if data
permit; do not call absence of Jev equivalent to absence of deterministic repair.

A causal ON/OFF comparison requires new, separately approved paired games with
same seeds, rosters, roles, prompts, thinking limits and memory; only repair mode
varies, after threshold freeze. Resample whole scenario blocks for Elo/rank-difference
intervals. Existing Elo with removed moves cannot provide that counterfactual.
No Elo estimate when the filtered comparison graph has no identifiable component.
Random fallback is a policy baseline, not assumed unbiased noise.

## Labeling/export contract for Coder

JSONL record fields: schema_version, case_id, source_game/match/decision/attempt ids,
scenario_block_id, game/prompt/model/config/repair hashes, eligible/exclusion_reason,
failure_type, visible_payload and hash, canonical_options (id/tool/exact arguments),
option_count/bucket, split, anonymized display mapping/seed, rater_id and independent
label (unique/ambiguous/unrecoverable, intended_option_id or null, evidence spans,
legal_intent, payload_leak), adjudication/version. Keep Jev predictions inaccessible
to labeling UI until labels lock; export predictions later in a separately joined
file keyed by case_id. Keep original attempts and before/after arguments intact.
Never turn a model-generated suggestion into a human label.

Referee export: match/scenario/moment ids, event-based selection reason, question
id/rubric version, visibility/view payload hash, deterministic truth result or
unknown, two independent labels and adjudication, raw Jev distribution/answer and
operational status, usage/cost/job id. Rater ids are pseudonyms; no personal data.
This is an interface spec only; Coder owns extraction/UI/export implementation.
