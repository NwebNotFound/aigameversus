# D-127 · When a Werewolf scene ends, the game master's line takes the stage until someone speaks

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decision:** At nightfall, morning, an execution, the captain's election and the end, the middle of the Werewolf circle shows a recap card. It has the scene's mark (moon, sunrise, scales, star, crown), a small label ("Nightfall", "Morning", "The vote", "The captain", "The end") and the game master's line in the narrator's serif.
  - **Text:** the line comes from the arena: the dramatic `narration` of the match analytics (#117, with display names), in the match's language. Without it (an older arena), the transcript's own narration line stands in.
  - **When it shows:** the card holds the stage until the next speech, and the speech then takes it back (D-115).
  - **Look:** gold by day, moonlit by night. It rises in with motion and appears without it under reduced motion.
  - **Fetching:** the circle fetches the analytics only when a narration is newer than what it has, so a replay fetches once and a live match once per scene.
- **Why:** Lead's Werewolf list asks for night and day recap cards. After a death or a vote the circle was silent, so the moment that matters most had no words on the board; the narrator's lines were importance 1 and hidden in the commentary.
- **Alternatives:** A separate banner above the board: it competes with the board, and on phones it pushes the circle down. Writing the recap in the web from the events: that would be a second formatter (CLAUDE.md forbids one), and the arena already writes these lines.
