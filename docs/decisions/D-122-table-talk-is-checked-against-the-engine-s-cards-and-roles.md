# D-122 · Table talk is checked against the engine's cards and roles, by rule

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decision:** `arena/analytics/talk.py` (`talk_checks`, `GET /matches/{id}/talk-checks`) reads what a player said about their own cards or role and compares it with the engine's ground truth: Liar's Deck ("I have no kings", "I'm being honest", "playing two kings" against the hand and the cards played), Poker ("I have a flush", "I have nothing", "pocket kings" against hole cards and board), Werewolf (role claims, taken from the claim analytics of #108/#117). Each result is a tag with the quote, the claim as read, `true`/`false`, the evidence and `heuristic: true`. No model is asked. Questions and statements about others are never checked. Without `spoilers` Liar's Deck and Poker give no tags, and Werewolf only the claims a death disproved. The tags are research data, not a verdict on a player; a sentence the patterns miss is not checked.
- **Why:** the engine knows the hands and roles, so a lie in talk is a statement that contradicts them, with no need for a judge. It is the falsifiable part of the Jev referee idea (JEV-FINDINGS.md) and gives the later referee a ground truth to be measured against. English only, like the prompts.
- **Alternatives:** asking a model (Jev or other) whether a statement is a lie (noisy and paid; kept for what the engine cannot decide); parsing every sentence (false positives; the patterns are narrow on purpose); showing hidden-card evidence in the spoiler-free view (a spoiler).
